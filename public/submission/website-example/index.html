Quality Groceries and Wholesale Supplies for Your Home and Business

Welcome to Sharma Daily Mart, your neighbourhood grocery and wholesale desk in Mansarovar, Jaipur, managed by Kavita Sharma. We supply daily household essentials and bulk provisions for local businesses. Browse our catalogue to request your daily staples, from fresh rice and dal to our special Family Grocery Hamper.

01

Daily Groceries & Hampers

We stock a wide range of daily essentials to keep your kitchen running smoothly. Choose from individual items or simplify your shopping with our Family Grocery Hamper, which includes rice, atta, dal, sugar, and tea. Please submit an enquiry to check current stock levels and confirm your pickup or delivery order.

02

Wholesale & Bulk Orders

Local businesses can rely on us for bulk supplies in approved case and sack quantities. Whether you need cafe supplies or wholesale sacks of staples, we are here to help. Credit facilities are available exclusively to previously approved business buyers against accepted invoices.

03

Delivery & Pickup Information

We deliver within a 3 km radius of our Mansarovar location. A minimum order value applies for delivery, with a small fee for standard orders and free delivery for larger purchases. You are also welcome to arrange a pickup during our regular opening hours. All orders and stock substitutions require owner confirmation.

GOOD TO KNOW

A few useful answers.

What are your store opening hours?

We are open daily from 08:00 to 21:00. You can arrange pickups or request deliveries during these hours.

How does local delivery work?

We deliver within 3 km of our store. There is a minimum order requirement and a standard delivery fee, but delivery is free for orders above a certain amount. Please check the catalogue for exact details.

Can I pick up my groceries immediately?

A pickup enquiry is not a confirmed pickup order. Please allow us to check stock and confirm your order before you visit. We will discuss availability and prepare your items once confirmed.

Do you offer discounts on wholesale purchases?

We supply wholesale items in specific case or sack quantities as listed in our catalogue. No additional discounts are approved on these orders.
