# KaamSet pitch and live demo

## 90-second pitch

An Indian service merchant can lose a booking between a customer question, a calendar check and the follow-up record. The customer experiences one conversation, but the merchant has to coordinate several tools and policies.

KaamSet gives that merchant an AI team with one customer-facing conversation. In our fictional Mumbai appliance business, the advisor answers from the merchant's approved price and service terms. When a customer asks for a time, the booking coordinator checks the live Calendar. It offers valid openings, creates the selected event and reads it back before saying the booking is confirmed. A fixed demo Sheet row records the same appointment. A change updates the existing event rather than creating another one.

The merchant can see the work and take control of the conversation. Guest access is limited to fictional business data, a dedicated demo Calendar and Sheet, and explicit usage limits. Payments are not connected. The judges can test the real workflow at the public URL without signing in.

Our business hypothesis is a subscription for service merchants that need reliable inquiry handling and appointment coordination. For Paytm's merchant ecosystem, a future version could pair that workflow with authorized commerce tools, subject to partner access and merchant consent. The product today demonstrates the teammate workflow and verified actions. We invite you to challenge it with a changed date, an unavailable area or a policy question.

## Three-minute live demo script

1. **0:00-0:20** Open <https://kaamset-web.vercel.app>. Point out the fictional merchant label. Launch Aamchi Appliance Care.
2. **0:20-0:55** Send: “My AC is not cooling. I’m in Andheri. What does a service include, and how much will it cost?” Confirm that the answer includes the approved diagnostic fee of ₹699 and does not ask for the area again.
3. **0:55-1:30** Send: “Book tomorrow after 4. Can we continue in Marathi?” Read out the live openings in Asia/Kolkata. Select an offered number.
4. **1:30-2:05** Switch to the merchant view. Show the confirmed booking, Calendar event ID, fixed Sheet state and evidence timeline. Explain that booked service value is not collected revenue.
5. **2:05-2:35** Return to customer view and ask: “Actually, move it an hour later.” Check that the event ID stays the same if the policy and availability allow the change.
6. **2:35-3:00** Show pause/takeover, the separate Harbour Appliance Works merchant, and the connections view. State that payment integration is not connected.

If tomorrow is a closed day or no live opening remains, choose the next operating day and say what changed. Do not present a replay as a fresh live booking.

## Suggested judge challenges

- Ask about a replacement part: the approved diagnostic fee does not include parts.
- Ask for service outside Andheri, Bandra, Dadar, Powai or Kurla.
- Ask to move a booking into a busy or closed period.
- Open a second guest session and confirm it cannot read the first session's messages or bookings.
- Pause the team in the merchant view and try to send a customer turn.
